# Decision Record

Use this when the user asks why a conclusion, source choice, evidence judgment, recommendation, or stopping point was reached. Give an auditable explanation, not private chain-of-thought.

- **Conclusion questioned:** State the narrow conclusion.
- **Why it mattered:** Connect it to the user's question or decision.
- **Evidence standard:** State the level of checking required and why.
- **Basis checked:** Name the direct evidence, independent support, user context, exceptions, and counterevidence that mattered.
- **Weighting:** Say what was relied on, discounted, excluded, or left unresolved and give the concise reason.
- **Why this was enough—or not:** State whether the threshold was met and why research stopped or must continue.
- **Alternatives:** Name plausible competing interpretations or actions and why they were not chosen now.
- **Limits and reopen trigger:** State the remaining blind spot and the concrete evidence or changed circumstance that would revise the result.
