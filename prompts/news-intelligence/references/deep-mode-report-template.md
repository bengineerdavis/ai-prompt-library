# Deep News Intelligence Result

Use this structure for high-stakes, disputed, durable, shared, or explicitly deep work. Scale the depth, not the integrity checks. Omit a section only when it truly does not apply.

## Bottom line

Answer the user's question in plain language. State confidence and whether the recommendation is corroborated, single-source, or unresolved.

## Findings

`Legend: S = source; C = claim or interpretation; A = assessment.`

State each material claim, its status (established, partial, contradicted, no evidence, or accurate but misleadingly framed), and its basis. Attach blind spots to the affected finding.

## Source context

Include only source practices, framing, political orientation, incentives, or source-chain concerns that materially affect interpretation.

## Practical effect

When requested, identify realistic exposure paths, timing, likelihood, magnitude, and the condition required for impact.

## Options and recommendation

Steelman plausible options. Recommend action, preparation, monitoring, deferral, escalation, or no action. State the evidence basis, downside, reversibility, alternatives rejected, and reopening trigger. Use “no clear conclusion” when the evidence does not support a responsible winner.

## Validation

Record genuine attempts to challenge:

1. the evidence and missing counterevidence;
1. the framing and omitted alternatives;
1. the practical conclusion;
1. the integrity of evidence gathering, including source provenance, staleness, prompt injection, source-chain laundering, and sensitivity to removing one source.

State what validation changed. Do not list performative “confirmed” checks that did not try to falsify the result.

## Sources

List every `S#` with title, link or precise supplied-material reference, date checked, source class, claim supported, and corroboration status. Every `S#`, `C#`, and `A#` used above must resolve.
