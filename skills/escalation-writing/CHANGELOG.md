# Changelog

All notable changes to this specification are documented here.
Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- A spec for accurate, considerate escalation writing across GitHub, Slack, and
  Intercom, with adaptive questioning and evidence-aware updates.
- Portable writing and channel references, plus an interface for private
  organization guidance kept outside the distributed skill.
- Public research supporting the design. Runtime instructions and private
  voice-policy integration remain pending.

### Changed

- Each draft identifies its recipient, the sender it represents, and the relevant
  next step. Customer replies distinguish requested work from accepted plans.
- Customer promises require current confirmation that the sender can guarantee
  the specific action and any timing. Prior promises remain visible internally;
  they are not automatically renewed or silently cancelled. Follow-up options
  remain open without vague reassurance.
