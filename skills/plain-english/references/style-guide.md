# Style guide references

The standard is the Google Developer Documentation Style Guide. We do not
mirror it; these links are the primary source. License: CC BY 4.0 (attribution
satisfied by linking the guide).

The three structure-and-vocabulary disciplines beyond Google (sentence caps,
one name per concept, word-level lockdown) are **inspired by Simplified
Technical English (ASD-STE100)** as studied in the internal research
`getsentry/ste-writing-research` — ideas only; that repository carries no
license and no text was copied. Its findings also temper rule 8: software
prose draws on three overlapping vocabularies (natural language,
software-engineering terms, and the product's own domain), so one-term-per-
concept binds within a concept, not across domains. Public STE overview:
<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Technical_English>.

Last verified: 2026-09-17.
